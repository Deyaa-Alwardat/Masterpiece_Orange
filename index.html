FAQ

Questions before
you start

Can’t find your answer? Write to hello@nexus.jo or contact the workspace directly.

Do I book a whole space or a specific desk?

NEXUS lets you choose a specific desk from the floor plan, depending on availability and zone.

What if I need a service while I’m inside?

Create a service request linked to your desk — Wi‑Fi, maintenance, hospitality or printing. It appears instantly on the space owner’s dashboard.

How does the AI Expert Matchmaker work?

Describe your problem in your own words. The system detects the relevant needs, compares them with expert specialties and availability, then shows suggestions you can choose from.

Can businesses book multiple desks?

Yes. Business Accounts are designed to manage team bookings, services and expert sessions from one account.
