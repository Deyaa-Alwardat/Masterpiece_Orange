Privacy, terms & cookies

This site is a demonstration of the NEXUS platform. The notes below describe how the demo behaves; they are not a binding legal agreement.

PrivacyTermsCookies

Privacy

This is a screens-only demo. Nothing you enter is stored, saved or sent anywhere — sample data is generated in your browser and disappears when you refresh.

The newsletter form stores your email address in your browser only. Clearing site data removes it.

Card details typed into the demo checkout are validated in the page and never stored; only the last four digits are kept to label a payment.

Terms of use

Bookings, payments, payouts and invoices in this environment are simulated. No money is charged or transferred, and invoices are not tax documents.

Demo sign-in passwords are hashed with a simple demo function and offer no real security. Do not enter real passwords or personal data.

A production launch will replace the demo layer with a real backend, real payment processing and legally reviewed terms.

Cookies & storage

NEXUS sets no cookies. It uses browser storage for your session, theme preference, demo data and remembered choices. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings, or reset the demo from the admin console.

Google Fonts are loaded from Google's servers to display the NEXUS typography.
